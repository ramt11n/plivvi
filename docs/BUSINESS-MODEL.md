# Plivvi: earn revenue while the tools stay free

Updated 6 November 2026. This is a business experiment, not a revenue forecast.

## Positioning

**Your PDF. Your device. Your call. Free and open source.**

Lead with document processing in the user's browser, no required account, public source and an installable responsive web app. Demonstrate the actual supported task. Do not lead with Persian/Arabic OCR, claim Acrobat-level editing, perfect scanning, universal Apple Pencil behavior or “100% secure.” Open source makes inspection possible; it is not a security audit. Optional accounts/cloud connections contact their providers; hosting receives normal request metadata.

## Revenue priorities

1. **Optional paid services, first.** The free app demonstrates the maker's work. Offer a small, separately scoped service: configure a document workflow for a print shop/tutor/team, adapt a template, or create a branded deployment. Keep every existing tool free for everyone. Paid work is time, support and customization, not a hidden paywall. Choose one deliverable and a written fixed scope; do not promise unlimited work.
2. **Direct, contextual sponsorship.** Sell one clearly labeled static placement on a public page or useful guide. Keep sponsors away from private document views, avoid third-party tracking code, and agree the duration and creative in writing. Start with a modest pilot only when you can show genuine usage. Do not invent traffic or promise impressions. Receive payment only through routes available to both parties; check provider and counterparty restrictions before contracting.
3. **Voluntary support.** Keep the existing crypto support route as a way to fund fixes. Ask after a successful task, honor dismissal and let the user finish exporting immediately. A donation is not a purchase, a promised feature or dependable recurring revenue. Don't keep interrupting the same visitor.
4. **Affiliate offers, selectively.** Only recommend something useful that you would still recommend without a commission. Clearly disclose the relationship and verify program/payment eligibility. Do not place competing offers in the middle of someone's document task.

Ads can be considered later with an eligible provider and enough actual audience. They are not the first-income plan. Google explicitly lists Iran as unavailable for AdSense. A VPN or a crypto address does not establish publisher/payment eligibility. Do not create an ineligible account or misstate location. This document does not assume that another ad network, sponsor or payment provider is available to the owner.

## What $100 could look like

Arithmetic examples only, before fees/taxes/costs, not expected conversion rates or recommended market prices:

| Model | Example that totals $100 | What must be true |
|---|---|---|
| Custom setup | Two separately scoped $50 jobs | Customers value the work and the scope fits the time |
| Direct sponsor | One $100 monthly pilot | Real audience fit, an available payment route and an agreed placement |
| Voluntary support | Twenty $5 contributions | People choose to support; not predictable |

A free user does not automatically become revenue. The app must solve a recurring problem, be easy to recommend, and give an interested supporter or customer an obvious contact route. For a first $100, a small number of paying service customers is a more controllable experiment than hoping for mass advertising traffic. It is still not guaranteed.

## Acquisition that can be sustained without a video budget

Start with one audience: students, teachers and freelancers who regularly combine, read or sign PDFs. Use English for the main international account; Persian content can explain the same useful workflows without making Persian OCR the selling point.

- **One short reel a week:** a concrete nuisance, a real Plivvi action, the finished result, one direct URL. Use animated type and real screenshots or recordings. The reusable renderer has no paid generation API.
- **Two carousels a week:** a concise tutorial and a privacy/PWA explanation. Keep the same typography, colors, layout and mascot. Reuse the same content as a story or a relevant reply.
- **Useful distribution:** the maker's own account, willing testers, and communities that permit product posts. Disclose authorship and answer actual questions. A launch on Show HN or Product Hunt is one experiment, not an ongoing acquisition engine. Never buy followers, coordinated votes or fake reviews.
- **Direct tool links:** the post about merging links to /merge-pdf/, not the broad homepage. The install/privacy introduction can link to the homepage. A caption should tell someone what they can finish, not only list features.

Batch content. One script and visual session should produce the reel, carousel, cover and caption. A human voice recording is optional. Do not pay for generative character video just to get moving letters or a UI zoom.

## First 30 days

**Week 1:** finish the privacy/free/PWA introduction; ask ten willing testers to complete one demonstrated PDF task. Fix the most common obstacle. Prepare one narrowly scoped service offer and a contact email.

**Week 2:** publish two task demonstrations and one practical guide post. Share where relevant and allowed. Personally answer replies. Use the private bug-report form to collect reproducible obstacles rather than private documents.

**Week 3:** invite a small number of genuinely relevant businesses to discuss a paid workflow pilot, with explicit outreach authorization before sending messages. Do not send a bulk campaign. Prepare a one-page sponsor offer with truthful audience data only if there is data to show.

**Week 4:** compare what visitors tried, where they got stuck, voluntary support, and actual customer inquiries. Choose the next task to improve and the best-performing format. Stop formats that take a lot of production time without useful engagement.

## Measure the path to value

Look for a successful export, return use, someone sharing the tool and a real inquiry. Likes alone are not the business. Without product analytics, use voluntary tester feedback and aggregate hosting/Search Console trends; hosting requests include bots and are not a count of users. Do not add document-content tracking. No new analytics SDK is enabled by this plan.

Keep a simple weekly ledger: published asset/link, aggregate visits where available, completed tester tasks, inquiries, supporters, gross revenue, fees and hours spent. Do not describe a financial break-even point until actual costs and income exist.

## Boundaries for future Instagram management

Prepare content in batches and obtain the actual account connection and posting scope when the owner is ready. Drafting assets is separate from posting, answering DMs, contacting sponsors or spending money. Do not share credentials in the repository. Prefer a supported account connection over password sharing. No Instagram account access, posts, DMs or ad spend have been activated by this document.
