# Share Plivvi

## Short introduction

Meet Plivvi: free, open-source PDF tools and digital notebooks in your browser. Merge, split, scan, convert, OCR, read, and annotate documents without a feature subscription. Create ruled, dotted, grid, Cornell, or blank notebooks; write with a compatible Apple Pencil or digital pen. Built by Ramtin Neshatvar, proudly vibe coded and open to contributions.

Try it: https://plivvi.pages.dev/
