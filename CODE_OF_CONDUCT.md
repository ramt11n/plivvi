# A comfortable place to contribute

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss the work without personal attacks, harassment, or publishing someone’s private information.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Explain disagreements with evidence and give maintainers time to respond. Maintainers may moderate content that disrupts the project or exposes private data.
